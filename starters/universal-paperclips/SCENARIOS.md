# Paperclip Maximizer

### UP-01 Make and sell clips using wire
- Given a new game
- When I make a clip and let the market run
- Then wire is consumed, inventory is sold, and cash increases.

### UP-02 Expand from the market to autonomous production
- Given a new game
- When I sell clips, purchase an AutoClipper, and research factories and probes
- Then production advances from market to industry to space and completes the game.
